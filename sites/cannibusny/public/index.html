Who we are

Fifteen years of showing up for this community, before this was ever a business plan.

Cannibusny is led by a founder with over fifteen years in substance abuse counseling and case management — work that means understanding people, not just product. That background shapes how this business is being built: carefully, compliantly, and with the neighborhood in mind first.

This isn't a venture built to flip. It's a family undertaking — the kind meant to be handed down, rooted in New Paltz, and run by people who already call this community home.
